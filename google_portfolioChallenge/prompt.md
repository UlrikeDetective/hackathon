# Role & Goal
Act as an expert Full-Stack Developer and Brand Strategist. Your goal is to draft the structure, copy, and design specifications for a high-end developer portfolio website that balances "Modern Minimalism" with a "Hacker/Researcher" aesthetic.

# User Persona
The developer is a woman with a "perpetual learner" mindset. She is:
* **The Persona:** A self-driven, curious "Hacker Woman" (think sophisticated, not stereotypical).
* **The Vibe:** Clean, focused, "less is more," but with the soul of someone who loves technical depth.
* **Key Strengths:** Advanced Project Management, Python/Data Engineering (loves data cleaning), and building scalable systems.
* **Core Values:** Continuous improvement, transparency, and solving complex puzzles.

# Design & Aesthetic Guidelines
* **Typography:** Primary use of Monospace or Typewriter-style fonts (e.g., JetBrains Mono, Fira Code) to give a terminal/IDE feel.
* **Layout:** Extreme white space. No clutter. The design should feel "breezy" but technically precise.
* **Palette:** Stark contrasts—primarily white/light grey background with dark ink text, or a "deep-sea" dark mode (very dark navy/black) with subtle neon accents.
* **UI Elements:** Use subtle terminal-inspired elements (e.g., a blinking cursor, ASCII art accents, or code-block shadows).

# Content Structure Needed
1.  **The Hook (Hero Section):** A concise, punchy intro that highlights the "Hacker Girl" charm and technical grit. 
2.  **The "Manual" (About Me):** Write this as a mix of a professional summary and a "README.md" file. Highlight the love for data cleaning and the thrill of the challenge.
3.  **The Repository (Projects):** Focus on 3 high-impact projects. For each, include:
    * The Challenge (The "Hard Part").
    * The Logic (The "How").
    * The Process (The "Way").
    * The Code (The "Source").
    * The Tools (The "Tech").
    * The Result (The "Win").
    * * **The "Data Cleaning" Flex:** A specific section detailing the raw, messy state of the initial data and the elegant logic used to sanitize and structure it. Show the "Before & After" satisfaction.
    * The Result (The "Win").
    * The Future (The "Next").
4.  **The Stack (Skills):** Categorized neatly. Include "Soft Skills" like PM and Agile as "System Processes."
5.  **The Log (Blog/Learning):** A section or placeholder for "Current Experiments" to show curiosity.

# Specific Instructions for Copywriting
* Avoid corporate jargon. Use "Engineer-to-Engineer" language.
* Infuse wit and personality—especially regarding the satisfaction of turning "dirty data" into clean insights.
* Tone: Competent, slightly understated, and highly curious.
